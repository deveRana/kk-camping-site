Booking & reservations

How do I book a stay?+

Pick a property from our Stays page, choose your dates and guest count, add any experiences, fill in your details, and pay. Instant confirmation. Total time: 60 seconds.

How far in advance can I book?+

Up to 4 months out. For dates further, WhatsApp us — we can usually block them off manually.

Can I book without paying online?+

Yes. For enquiries or group bookings, WhatsApp us on +91 98765 43210 and we'll block dates and share payment options.

Is my booking confirmed instantly?+

Yes for all listings marked "Instant book" (which is most of them). You'll get an SMS, WhatsApp message, and email within 60 seconds with the voucher.

The stay

What's included in the price?+

Every stay includes: accommodation, evening tea & snacks, BBQ dinner, bonfire, breakfast, and access to lake-view areas. Some activities are add-ons.

What are check-in and check-out times?+

Check-in from 12:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out subject to availability — ask your host.

Do I need to carry ID?+

Yes. All adult guests need a valid government photo ID at check-in (Aadhaar, DL, PAN, Passport, or Voter ID). It's a Maharashtra regulation, not our rule.

Is alcohol allowed?+

You can bring your own. Some properties sell it, some don't — check the individual listing. Please drink responsibly around the water and bonfire.

Are pets allowed?+

Some properties are pet-friendly with a ₹500/night fee. Filter by "Pet-friendly" on the Stays page or ask us.

Payment & pricing

What payment methods do you accept?+

UPI (all apps), credit/debit cards (Visa, MC, RuPay, Amex), net banking, and wallets (Paytm, Amazon Pay). Balance amount can also be paid in cash on arrival.

Can I pay in installments?+

Yes. Choose "Pay 30% now, rest on arrival" at checkout. No interest, no fees.

Are the prices per person or per night?+

Tents are usually per person per night. Villas and cottages are per night (whole property). Check the listing — it's labeled clearly on each card.

Any hidden fees?+

No. Cleaning fee (₹200) and service fee (₹150) are shown before you pay. GST (18%) applies. That's it.

Cancellation & refunds

What's the cancellation policy?+

Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. 50% refund up to 3 days before. No refund within 72 hours — but you can reschedule up to 6 months out. Full details: Cancellation Policy.

How long do refunds take?+

3–5 business days to your original payment method. UPI refunds are usually instant.

Can I reschedule instead of cancel?+

Yes — usually a better option. Reschedule for free up to 3 days before check-in. After that, one reschedule per booking with ₹200 fee.

Groups & corporate

Do you handle corporate offsites?+

Yes — from 10 to 100+ people. Dedicated coordinator, custom pricing, team-building activities, and GST invoicing. WhatsApp us for a quote.

Can we book out a whole property?+

Yes. Whole-property bookings for villas, farmhouses, and camps are common — bulk pricing kicks in from 10+ guests.

Getting there

How do I reach Pawna Lake?+

From Pune (~2 hrs): via Talegaon or Kamshet. From Mumbai (~2.5 hrs): via Mumbai-Pune Expressway, exit at Kamshet. Nearest station: Kamshet. We share exact directions on WhatsApp after booking.

Do you offer pickup from the station?+

From Kamshet — yes, at cost (~₹400 one way). Ask your host after booking.

Activities & add-ons

What activities are available?+

Kayaking, sunrise treks, boat rides, archery, carrom, cricket, live music, and campfire. See full details when booking your stay.

Can I book activities after arrival?+

Most, yes — but book kayaking and treks in advance since spots are limited on weekends.
