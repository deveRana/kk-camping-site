Frequently asked

How far is Pawna Lake from Pune and Mumbai? +

Pawna Lake is about 2 hours from Pune (60 km via Talegaon) and 2.5 hours from Mumbai (100 km via Expressway). We share exact directions on WhatsApp after booking.

What's included in the stay price? +

Every stay includes accommodation, evening tea & snacks, BBQ dinner, bonfire, breakfast, and unlimited lake views. Activities like kayaking are optional add-ons.

Can I book for a large group or corporate offsite? +

Yes. We handle groups from 10 to 100+ with custom pricing, dedicated coordinators, team-building activities, and private property bookings. WhatsApp us for a group quote.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. 50% refund up to 3 days before. No refund within 72 hours, but we can reschedule to any date within 6 months.

Are pets allowed? +

Some of our properties are pet-friendly — mention it during booking and we'll match you with the right stay. Extra ₹500/night pet fee.
