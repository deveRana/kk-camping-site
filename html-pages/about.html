Lakeora started with a group of friends who kept escaping the city to Pawna Lake — and kept wishing someone would make it easier for everyone else.

How it started

In 2020, three friends from Pune drove to Pawna for a weekend. Cell signal died. Phones went in bags. Bonfire got lit. Nobody wanted to leave.

When we tried to plan a second trip — for a bigger group this time — it was chaos. Twenty tabs open, sketchy WhatsApp bookings, no clear pricing, and photos that looked nothing like reality.

So we built the thing we wished existed: a curated collection of lakeside stays we've personally slept in, with honest photos, transparent pricing, and hosts we know by name.

What we care about

Our values

Honest photos

Every image is real, taken by us or the host — never stock. If it's not in the photo, it's not on the property.

No hidden fees

The price you see is the price you pay. Every fee broken down before checkout.

Local hosts

Every property is family-owned. Your money stays in Pawna — not with a middleman.

Leave it better

Every stay funds our lakeshore cleanup and tree-planting programs. So far: 4,200 kg trash removed.
