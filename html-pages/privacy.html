LakeStay Hospitality Pvt. Ltd. ("LakeStay", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are. It applies to lakestay.in, our mobile app, and all related services.

1. Information we collect

1.1 You give us

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, WhatsApp number
  • Booking details: dates, guest count, special requests, arrival time
  • Government ID: only at check-in, for regulatory compliance (Maharashtra tourism rules)
  • Payment info: processed by our PCI-DSS certified payment partners — we don't store card numbers
  • Communications: emails, WhatsApp messages, support tickets

1.2 Collected automatically

  • IP address, browser, device type, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, referring URL
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see §4)

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process bookings and payments
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and updates
  • Provide guest support
  • Improve our services (aggregated analytics, not individual profiling)
  • Send marketing emails or WhatsApp messages — only if you opted in
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax, regulatory)

3. Sharing your information

We share your information only with:

  • Host properties: name, phone, arrival time, guest count, special requests — so they can host you
  • Payment processors: Razorpay, Stripe (for payment processing)
  • Communication tools: MSG91 (SMS), Twilio (WhatsApp), SendGrid (email)
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4, PostHog (with IP anonymization)
  • Legal authorities: only when required by law

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or brokers.

4. Cookies

We use:

  • Essential cookies: to keep you logged in and remember your cart
  • Analytics cookies: to understand how the site is used
  • Marketing cookies: only if you opt in (Meta Pixel, Google Ads)

You can manage cookies from your browser settings or from our cookie preferences page.

5. Security

We use TLS 1.3 for all data in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Access to production data is restricted to a small on-call team, gated by 2FA and audited. Payment data is handled entirely by PCI-DSS Level 1 processors. That said — no system is 100% secure. If we ever detect a breach affecting you, we'll tell you within 72 hours.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention needs)
  • Nominate someone to act on your behalf
  • File a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

Email privacy@lakestay.in — we respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary:

  • Booking records — 7 years (for tax and accounting)
  • Account data — until you ask us to delete
  • Marketing consent — until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics — 14 months (Google Analytics default)

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, please contact us at privacy@lakestay.in and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email to active users. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.
