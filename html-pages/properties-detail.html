About this stay

Wake up to birdsong and the smell of chai brewing over a wood fire. Our Deluxe Tents are pitched on a private clearing 120 metres from Pawna Lake, spaced out enough that you never feel crowded but close enough to hear the water.

Each tent has a proper queen bed, cotton linens, an attached bathroom with hot water, and a small sit-out with a view. Evenings are around the bonfire with BBQ, music, and stories. Perfect for couples, small friend groups, or anyone who wants nature without giving up comfort.
