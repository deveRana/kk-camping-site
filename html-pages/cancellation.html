How to cancel

Two ways:

  1. Go to My Bookings → find the trip → click "Cancel"
  2. WhatsApp us on +91 98765 43210 with your Booking ID

Cancellation timestamp is when we receive your request, not when it's processed.

Refund timelines

Payment method Refund time
UPI Instant to 24 hours
Credit / Debit card 3–5 business days
Net banking 3–5 business days
Wallet (Paytm, etc.) 24–48 hours

Refunds go to your original payment method. Bank/wallet delays are outside our control.

Rescheduling

Change your mind? Reschedule instead of cancel — often a better deal.

  • Free reschedule up to 3 days before check-in
  • Within 3 days: one reschedule per booking with ₹200 admin fee
  • New dates must be within 6 months of the original
  • If new dates cost more, pay the difference. If less, we refund the difference.

Cancellation by Lakeora

In the rare case we cancel your booking (major weather, safety issues, property emergency), you get a 100% refund plus a ₹500 credit toward your next booking. We'll also help you find alternatives.

Special situations

Monsoon weather waivers

If the Maharashtra Meteorological Department issues a red alert for your dates, we refund fully or reschedule — regardless of tier.

Medical emergency

With a hospital admission letter, we waive cancellation charges regardless of timing. Contact us within 48 hours of the emergency.

Bereavement

Full refund with a supporting document. No time limit.

Group and corporate bookings

Groups of 10+ have a separate cancellation schedule, agreed at time of booking. Typically: full refund 21+ days before, 50% between 14–21 days, no refund inside 14 days.
